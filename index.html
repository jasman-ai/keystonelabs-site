A software house registered in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. We build tools that let a small business run its own marketing — without hiring an agency and without learning a design program.

What we build

Most small businesses know they should be posting. Very few have an hour a day to spend on it, and the software built for this problem assumes a marketing team that a café or a barber shop does not have.

Our products start from the opposite assumption: the work is already done when the owner opens the app. Their job is to say yes, or send it back. Everything else — the writing, the pictures, the schedule, the publishing to their own accounts — is ours.
